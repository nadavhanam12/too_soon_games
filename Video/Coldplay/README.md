# Too Soon Games — Coldplay process film

60 seconds, 1280×720, 24 fps, Hebrew, 16:9. Illustrated motion graphics with gentle camera movement, animated camera framing and traction counters, Hebrew captions, and an original synthesized music bed.

Final narrated version: `Too-Soon-Games-60s.mp4`, including Microsoft Hebrew Avri narration generated after the user's explicit approval to send the script. Each of the eight voice clips is fitted to its scene and mixed above a quieter music bed. The previous `Too-Soon-Games-60s-captioned.mp4` remains available as a music-and-captions version.

## Story

- 0–7 s: Gal's event pipeline discovers the actual July 2025 Coldplay audience-camera moment. Real event photograph appears with a source credit.
- 7–15 s: Gal, Nadav and Or choose an event together in a remote call.
- 15–30 s: Nadav and Or build and plan promotion together from separate locations. Fictional game characters hide from the camera.
- 30–38 s: Game and social clips launch at hour 12.
- 38–48 s: Hour 24: illustrative targets of 1,000,000 content views and 20,000 game visits. These are explicitly labeled as a success scenario, not observed results.
- 48–56 s: Hour 48: fictional news coverage, remote interview and mute joke. The news segment is explicitly labeled fictional.
- 56–60 s: The next event arrives.

Names appear visually only. Narration uses collective language. The time display is elapsed time since the event, not a deadline countdown.

## Assets and provenance

- `story-art.png`: artwork generated with the built-in image generation tool, then corrected to show Nadav and Or working in separate places. Generic fictional characters, not likenesses of the real team.
- `event-source.jpg`: actual concert-camera photograph retrieved from ABC News/GMA. Source URLs are recorded in `sources.json`; the video includes an attribution. No actual reporting about Too Soon Games is claimed.
- `scenes.json`: exact Hebrew script, titles, timings and elapsed-time labels.
- Music: original locally synthesized plucked-note progression with soft percussion, generated by `render.py`; no commercial music or Coldplay recording.
- `prepare.py`: narration preparation via Microsoft speech, only to run after explicit approval to send the script. No repository files are uploaded.

## Rebuild

Dependencies are isolated in the repository's ignored `.video-tools/` directory: Pillow, numpy, python-bidi, imageio-ffmpeg, and edge-tts. Python 3.12 and Windows Arial fonts were used.

`python Video/Coldplay/render.py --preview` creates inspection frames.

`python Video/Coldplay/render.py` creates a 60-second MP4. When all eight approved narration clips exist in `build/`, it mixes narration with music. Otherwise it creates the clearly named `Too-Soon-Games-60s-captioned.mp4` with music and captions only. The audio report records which version was created.
