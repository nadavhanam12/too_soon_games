**A plane is going down. You have five stages to save it.**

Not On My Flight is a free browser game from Too Soon Games. The player runs down the aisle of a diving airliner, breaks into the cockpit, stops the attacker and levels the plane, in five short arcade stages. The family in the game is fictional and the attacker and captain are generic; the facts screen at the end gives the details of the real flight the game is based on. It plays on any phone without signup and ends with a share link that challenges a friend to beat your score.

- Free, no signup, plays in any mobile or desktop browser
- Five short arcade stages, global leaderboard, friend-challenge links
- Fictional characters; the facts screen covers the real event
- Made by Too Soon Games, a small studio that turns news moments into games
