## Press

**Nvidia's CEO resigned. Now you can play the fall.**

Drop the Chip is a free browser game from Too Soon Games, built in the days after the November 2026 Nvidia CEO resignation. The player keeps a generic chip-company CEO falling down a tower of headlines while the board meeting (the floor) rises to catch him. Runs last about a minute, work on any phone without signup, and end with a share link that challenges a friend to beat your time. It is a parody: the character is generic, and the facts screen at the end links to the real reporting.

- Free, no signup, plays in any mobile or desktop browser
- About one minute per run, global leaderboard, friend-challenge links
- Built in about a day by Too Soon Games, a small studio that turns news moments into games
- Parody; the facts screen cites the Nvidia newsroom, Washington Post and Yahoo Finance
