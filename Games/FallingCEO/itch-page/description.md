# 📉 Drop the Chip

**Friday morning.** The CEO of a chip giant admits an affair. She is, allegedly, a spy.
The stock starts to fall. **So does he.**

A hand-drawn arcade parody. Icy Tower, but *downwards*: bounce on GPU chips, climb on whatever the news cycle throws at you, and stay above the floor. The floor is the resignation letter, and it is coming up fast.

**In real life it took 5 days. How long can you last?**

---

## 🎮 How to play

- **Hold the left / right side** of the screen (or ← → / A D) to move.
- **Tap JUMP** (or Space / ↑ / W) for one extra mid-air jump after each landing.
- Land on **GPU chips** to bounce. Only special platforms gain real height.
- **Don't touch the floor.** Every headline pushes it closer.

Plays in the browser, on phone or desktop. One hand is enough. About 2 minutes per run.

## ⚠️ Watch out for

| Platform | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **GPU chips** (H100, B200, Blackwell, Rubin…) | Your bread and butter. A normal bounce. |
| 🟧 **Trump** – *"He's an honorable guy."* | The biggest bounce in the game. |
| ✉️ **Apology letters** | Medium bounce, cameras flash. Three of them earn one golden **BOARD: FULL CONFIDENCE** platform. |
| 📰 **Newspapers** | Breaking headlines. They tear the moment you land. No bounce. |
| 💘 **The Agent & the VP** | Touch one and it's a date: a huge jump, but she sticks to you and every headline raises the floor. An apology letter sends her away. |
| 📈 **Strong Buy** / 🎤 **Keynote** / 🖥️ **DGX rack** | Special boosts. Grab them while they last. |

The CEO's face follows the market cap. Watch him go from **$5T** to *"I did not get to write this. Someone else did."*

## 🏆 Score

Your score is **seconds survived**. Each run ends with a resignation letter, a red stamp, and a rank.

Beat your best, then share your time with the group. 📲

## ℹ️ About

Made in a few days, **too soon**, as a reaction to the November 2026 Nvidia news cycle.
Everything is drawn in code: no sprites, no engine, one HTML file.

> This game is a parody. The character is fictional. The spy allegation is unverified.
> Sources are listed in-game under *"What actually happened"*.
