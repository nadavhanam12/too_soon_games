# TSG: Nice City (GameId WastedForSix, slug nice-city)

Renamed twice on 2026-11-17: first "Calling In Wasted" (calling-in-wasted), then "TSG: Nice City" (nice-city). The GameId (folder, leaderboard key) stays WastedForSix.

English (`us`) parody game about the GTA 6 launch day (2026-11-19): an ordinary fan who waited 13 years gets free from
work, gets the game, gets home and types the password. Built on `GamesTemplate/`; read `GamesTemplate/docs/RULES.md`
first. Brief, decisions and humor seeds: `research.md` (idea A, sections 6A / 7 / 10 / 11).

**Real brand (yellow, approved 2026-11-17):** "GTA 6", "Rockstar", "Take-Two" appear ONLY in `content.facts`. In-game it
is "the game", "VI" (never "Six", Nadav 2026-11-17), "launch day". WASTED is drawn in our own 5x7 pixel font (`src/pixel.ts`), never the real game's
typography. No sprites, HUD, logo or font copied; the top-down pixel look is an original homage.

## Story / flow (one `play` scene, seven stages, DECISIONS 40)

```
commute (drive home → office) → boss (gray scene) → launch (drive office → detour → store) → clerk (gray scene)
→ home (chase store → home) → fiancee (gray scene) → pc (type WASTED) → PRESS START beat (1.5 s, rule 13ג) → R.win
```
Each conversation (boss / clerk / fiancee) is built up (2026-11-18): heartbeat + red edges on the last stretch of the
drive, then its mini game (cough / line / tiptoe), then the VS card, then the conversation (`buildup.ts`, `minigames.ts`).
Later on 2026-11-18 (Nadav): one opening line per scene (no random pools); the conversation shows the whole portrait
with the speech and answers over its lower part (`portraits.ts` `drawBigPortrait`); LEVEL UP lists each upgrade on its
own line with a green UP mark (`content.ts` `LEVELUP.ups`) and leaves to black, so no frame of the old scene shows before
the montage; the montage's smash takes twice as long (`CRACK_AT` 0.5, `SMASH_AT` 1.5, three knocks) and ends in confetti
(`montage.ts` `celebrate`); after LEVEL UP VI the result screen comes at once (the PRESS START beat is gone); the PC's
login screen sits on a desk (`lazy/sets3d.ts` `buildDesk`). Driving legs: the UI enters part by part (`play.ts`
`UI_IN`), messages wait until it has settled (`UI_SETTLE_S`, `sayQueue`) and stay up for `readS`; the hint wraps inside
its box above the radio (template rule 13יד). PATIENT ZERO: three coughs in the lungs, one back every 1.5 s
(`PZ_LUNGS`, `PZ_REFILL_S`), droplets fly and splat on the floor (`spray`, `stepDrops`). The line: 95 u (~16 s), a higher
camera looking down, passed campers yell (`MINI.line.passed`, pinned to the screen edge low down, `SHOUT_MAX` 2). The
living room's TV shows an ad with the game case ("VI is coming!", `sets3d.ts` `tvAd`, `EnvironmentAssets.cover`). Then: the line has three painted lane lines (one per lane, `sets3d.ts` `buildLine`), no black square on the tents, big glass ◀ ▶ keys; on a phone the conversation stands on black, no road under the answers (`scenes.ts` `drawDecision`); how-to lines mark keys in [brackets], drawn yellow (`minigames.ts` `keyLines`, template `rich`). Every drive starts with a read pause (`legs.ts` `startRead`: `READ_S` 1 s dimmed `READ_DIM` 0.55 with the hint large in the middle, then `READ_FADE_S` 0.5 s; the chase home waits until its "who is after you" band has left, drawn over the dimming via `D.reading`); the hint is no longer drawn on the road. After the password: the finale (`finale.ts`: sunburst, fireworks, confetti cannons, the VI case flying in via `item.ts` `drawCase`, GAME ON!, CONTINUE) before LEVEL UP VI. Touch driving is the one-thumb pad only (the two-thumb controls and the switch were removed, Nadav).
- **boss:** agree → in-scene WASTED card, then the scene again without that option (`G.agreed`) · fake sick → branch
  `sick` · quit → branch `quit`, money → $0.
- **launch, sick:** forced clinic stop (beat "BRIBING", takes ALL money → sick note). HR minivan roams (biased toward
  you); inside its red circle before the note = fail `fired`; after = toast.
- **launch, quit:** fuel starts at 62% and drains 1/50 per second; empty = fail `fuel`. Forced gas station stop (beat
  "PUMPING", no gray scene) → 0.9 s later a cop spawns, 1 star. The cop's position is saved on store entry (`G.copPos`).
- **clerk:** loan → loan-shark sedan · steal → cop (+1 star) · trade-in → console / couch refused (option removed),
  ring → spouse minivan. Both branches arrive with $0.
- **home:** chaser per store choice; quit branch adds the gas-theft cop resumed from `G.copPos` (≥300 px away). Caught =
  fail (`shark` / `cop` / `spouse`). Chasers sit still for 1.2 s after the banner.
- **fiancee:** she is on the couch between you and the PC (opens with "Where is the ring" after the ring trade).
  Tiptoe past → refused, option removed · fake listening → "what did I just say?" quiz, every answer is wrong, option
  removed · asking honestly for one hour → on to the PC; +300 if it was your first pick (`scenes.ts` `HONESTY_BONUS`).
  Cannot be lost. Portraits `fiancee-idle / ring / caught / quiz / ok` (added 2026-11-17, ~105 KB each): a modern PC
  behind her shows WASTED in yellow pixel letters on black (a password hint).
- **pc:** on-screen QWERTY + physical keys, auto-submit at 6 letters. Wrong tries: joke + "n/6 letters in the right
  spot"; every wrong try gives one more letter, already typed in its slot in yellow and not deletable (`scenes.ts` `given`; W, then A, S…; at most 5), so the player only types the rest. From the first wrong try on, the bottom of the monitor shows `content.PC.caughtHint` in yellow instead of the rotating tips. First try = +500.
- Password hints: lose heading WASTED; graffiti letters W·A / S·T / E·D painted on each leg's roads (HUD shows found
  letters in password order); "waste" lines (boss, clerk, phone text); sticky notes on the PC.

Retry from a stage restores the snapshot taken when the stage started (`G.entry`: score, money, letters, note, fuel,
stars, copPos, refused trade items). `G.agreed` survives retries on purpose.

### Blender hero / environment integration

See `GRAPHICS-INTEGRATION.md` and `BLENDER-HANDOFF.md`. `src/models/hero.ts` consumes Blender-generated geometry and switches car paint/chrome properties by look without making skin/cloth metallic. Renderer `create(city, assets)` receives versioned environment URLs from `drive.ts` via `assetUrl`; textures in `assets/environment/` (~28KB total) load on first Look 3/4, with procedural fallbacks. Curved opaque palms are merged by 112-unit chunks; ledges/benches appear only in nearby Look 4 chunks. Touch-first devices use 1024 shadow maps and no MSAA; desktop retains 2048/4x. No new real-time lights or SSR. `__graphics()` reports render counters, CPU submit time, asset state and driver/walker visibility; it is not GPU timing. For the local QA panel, copy `tools/graphics-check.html` to `GamesTemplate/.local-dist/graphics-check.html` after a local build. Do not ship that test panel. The renderer disposes retired traffic/heli GPU buffers while preserving shared hero and beam assets.
