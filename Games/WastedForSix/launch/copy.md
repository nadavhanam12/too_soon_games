## Press

**A free parody game about getting GTA 6 launch day off work**

TSG: Nice City is a free browser game from Too Soon Games, made for the launch of GTA 6 on November 19, 2026, thirteen years after GTA V. You play an ordinary fan who still has a job on launch day: call in sick or quit, outrun HR, the police or the loan sharks across a 90s-style city, get a day-one copy, sneak past your fiancée and get home to play first. It plays on any phone or computer with no signup, and every run ends on a global leaderboard. It is an unofficial parody, not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two.

- Free, no signup, plays in any mobile or desktop browser
- Seven stages: three drives across one city, three mini-games, one password
- Two ways past the boss (call in sick or quit) and three ways to pay at the store
- Unofficial parody, not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two
- Made by Too Soon Games, a small studio that turns the news into games
